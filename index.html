Frequently Asked

Super short, simple answers to your island travel questions.

1. Passport needed for Indians?
No. Only Aadhar card, Voter ID, or Driving License is needed.
2. Best time to visit?
October to May offers calm blue seas and best underwater visibility.
3. Swimming required for scuba?
No. Certified PADI dive masters hold and guide you constantly.
4. How long does ferry take?
Port Blair to Havelock takes roughly 90 minutes via high-speed catamaran.
5. Can I visit your Pamgarh office?
Yes, our registered office is on Main Road, Pamgarh (Pincode: 495554).
6. What if sea weather is bad?
Our on-ground staff re-books next ferry or adjusts hotels at zero cost.
7. Are cabs private or shared?
All our cabs are 100% private, sanitized, and air-conditioned.
8. Advance booking token needed?
Only 20% advance token locks your complete itinerary and ferry passes.
9. Are vegetarian meals available?
Yes, pure veg, Jain, and North/South Indian restaurants are abundant.
10. Cellular Jail open on Mondays?
Cellular Jail is closed on Mondays and national holidays.
